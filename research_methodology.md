# EcoFusion: Research Methodology, Academic Validation & Experimental Plan

## 1. Research Problem Formulation & Gap Analysis

### 1.1 Formal Problem Statement
Given a set of $N$ cloud workloads $\mathcal{W} = \{w_1, \dots, w_N\}$, a set of $M$ geographically distributed data center resource pools $\mathcal{P} = \{p_1, \dots, p_M\}$, and a discretized scheduling horizon $\mathcal{T} = \{0, 1, \dots, H-1\}$ of $H = 24$ hourly slots:
- Each workload $w \in \mathcal{W}$ is characterized by arrival time $a_w \in [0, H-1]$, required CPU cores $c_w > 0$, required memory $m_w > 0$ (GB), execution duration $d_w \in \mathbb{Z}^+$ (hours), and hard SLA deadline $\text{deadline}_w > a_w$.
- Each resource pool $p \in \mathcal{P}$ has CPU capacity $C_{\text{CPU}}(p)$, memory capacity $C_{\text{MEM}}(p)$, base cooling efficiency $\text{PUE}_{\text{base}}(p)$, server idle/max power $[P_{\text{idle}}(p), P_{\text{max}}(p)]$, baseline grid carbon intensity $\text{CI}_{\text{base}}(p)$, and baseline electricity tariff $\text{Tariff}_{\text{base}}(p)$.

The orchestrator seeks a mapping $\pi: \mathcal{W} \to (\mathcal{P} \times \mathcal{T}) \cup \{\text{UNSCHEDULED}\}$ that simultaneously minimizes:
$$\min_{\pi} \mathbf{F}(\pi) = \bigl[\; f_{\text{Carbon}}(\pi),\; f_{\text{Energy}}(\pi),\; f_{\text{Cost}}(\pi),\; f_{\text{SLA}}(\pi) \;\bigr]^T$$
subject to hard arrival, horizon, and per-hour multi-resource capacity constraints across all $p \in \mathcal{P}$ and $h \in \mathcal{T}$.

### 1.2 Why Single-Objective Baselines Fail (The Research Gap)
In multi-region cloud grids, environmental and economic signals are **anti-correlated across space and time**:
- **Carbon vs. Cost Anti-Correlation:** Clean grids with lower carbon intensity (`POOL-SIN` Singapore, $270\text{ gCO}_2/\text{kWh}$) charge higher industrial electricity tariffs ($\$0.165/\text{kWh}$, rising $1.40\times$ during peak hours), whereas cheaper regions (`POOL-BOM` Mumbai, $\$0.085/\text{kWh}$ with $0.60\times$ night off-peak discount) rely heavily on night baseload coal ($620 \times 1.22 = 756.4\text{ gCO}_2/\text{kWh}$).
- **Deferred Green Shifting vs. SLA Contention:** Deferring flexible batch workloads to midday solar windows (`09:00–15:00`) or night off-peak windows (`22:00–05:00`) concentrates resource utilization into narrow temporal bands. When latency-critical workloads ($\text{slack} \in \{0, 1\}\text{ h}$) arrive during those windows, single-objective greedy schedulers suffer capacity bottlenecks and SLA violations.

## 3. Executed Ablation Studies & Remaining Experimental Plan

Priority 1 (NSGA-II Evolutionary Ablation) and Priority 2 (ML Urgency-Ordering Ablation) have been **executed and verified** across all 5 seeds (`42, 101, 202, 303, 404`) via [tests/run_ablation_experiments.cjs](file:///c:/VSCODE%20PROJECTS/EcoFusion/tests/run_ablation_experiments.cjs) (full tables in Sections 6 & 7 of [experimental_results.md](file:///c:/VSCODE%20PROJECTS/EcoFusion/experimental_results.md)):

### 3.1 Executed Priority 1: NSGA-II Evolutionary Ablation (`g = 0` vs. `g = 8`)
- **Finding 1 (Heuristic Warm-Start vs. Single-Pass):** A single balanced heuristic chromosome (`GEN0_SINGLE_BALANCED`, $p=0, g=0$) achieves a Composite Score of $66.44 \pm 3.77$ with $3.04 \pm 1.67\%$ SLA violations. Initializing the 14-individual directional population $P_0$ (`GEN0_HEURISTIC_POP`, $g=0$) improves the Composite Score to $71.34 \pm 3.50$ ($1.68 \pm 1.11\%$ SLA violations) with $8.60 \pm 1.36$ distinct non-dominated solutions.
- **Finding 2 (Pure Evolutionary Gain from Random Init):** When warm-start heuristic seeds are disabled (`GEN0_STOCHASTIC_POP` $\to$ `GEN8_STOCHASTIC_NSGA`), 8 generations of tournament selection, uniform crossover, and guided mutation improve the Composite Score by **$+3.36$ points ($66.68 \pm 1.66 \to 70.04 \pm 3.51$)** and expand distinct $\mathcal{F}_1$ solutions from $9.80 \pm 0.98$ to $14.00 \pm 0.00$.
- **Finding 3 (Full Hybrid NSGA-II at $g = 8$):** Evolving the hybrid population for 8 generations (`FULL_NSGA2_ML_GEN8`) achieves **$72.84 \pm 4.67$** ($+1.50$ points over $P_0$ at $g=0$, winning 4 of 5 seeds; $+6.40$ points over the single-pass heuristic), lowers mean energy by $76.76\text{ kWh}$, reduces SLA violations to $1.64 \pm 1.08\%$, and expands $\mathcal{F}_1(P_{\text{final}})$ to **$14.00 \pm 0.00$ distinct Pareto solutions** at an execution cost of $936.74 \pm 84.09\text{ ms}$ (vs. $226.18 \pm 61.73\text{ ms}$ at $g=0$).

### 3.2 Executed Priority 2: Machine Learning Urgency-Ordering Ablation (`ML Urgency` vs. `Pure Deadline` vs. `FIFO`)
- **FIFO Ordering (`FULL_NSGA2_FIFO_GEN8`):** Decodes workloads in chronological arrival order $(a_w, \text{id}_w)$. Early flexible batch jobs ($a_w \in [03:00, 06:00]$) occupy morning capacity, causing strict-deadline morning surge jobs to miss their deadlines (**SLA violations jump $4.5\times$ to $7.40 \pm 1.39\%$**, Composite Score drops to **$65.42 \pm 3.82$**).
- **Pure Deadline Ordering without ML (`FULL_NSGA2_DEADLINE_GEN8`):** Sorts strictly by $(\text{deadline}_w, a_w)$ without using Random Forest predictions $\hat{d}_w$ or $\hat{c}_w$. Reduces SLA violations to **$2.36 \pm 0.84\%$** and improves the Composite Score to **$69.84 \pm 3.58$** ($+4.42$ points over FIFO).
- **Random Forest Urgency Ordering (`FULL_NSGA2_ML_GEN8`):** Incorporates Random Forest predicted duration $\hat{d}_w$ and CPU $\hat{c}_w$ into $U_w = 0.65\,\text{dl}_w + 0.35(\text{dl}_w - a_w - \hat{d}_w)$ with energy tie-breaking $\hat{c}_w \cdot \hat{d}_w$. Further reduces SLA violations to **$1.64 \pm 1.08\%$**, reduces electricity cost by $\$54.80$ vs. Pure Deadline, and achieves the highest Composite Score (**$72.84 \pm 4.67$**, $+3.00$ points over Pure Deadline and $+7.42$ points over FIFO).

### 3.3 Remaining Future Extensions (Priorities 3–5)
- **Priority 3 — Workload Scale Sensitivity (`N = 500, 750, 1,000`):** Multi-seed scaling evaluation across the 3 preset dataset sizes.
- **Priority 4 — Hyperparameter Sensitivity ($N_{\text{pop}}$, $G_{\max}$, $p_m$):** One-factor-at-a-time sweep over population size, generation count, and mutation rate.
- **Priority 5 — Grid & SLA Strictness Sensitivity:** Parametric sweep over renewable penetration and zero-slack workload ratios.
